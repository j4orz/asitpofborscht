三月七日，沙湖道中遇雨。雨具先去，同行皆狼狈，余独不觉。已而遂晴，故作此词。

莫听穿林打叶声，<br>
何妨吟啸且徐行。<br>
竹杖芒鞋轻胜马，<br>
谁怕？<br>
一蓑烟雨任平生。<br>

料峭春风吹酒醒，<br>
微冷，<br>
山头斜照却相迎。<br>
回首向来萧瑟处，<br>
归去，<br>
也无风雨也无晴。<br>

</div>

<div>

On the seventh of the third month, we were caught in the rain along the Shahu Way without any rain gear. Everyone felt awkward, except myself. The sky later cleared, and I wrote these lyrics:

Heed not the rain beating against the leaves<br>
Why not whistle and sing as you stroll along<br>
Straw sandals and a bamboo staff <br>
are lighter and better than horseback<br>
What’s there to fear?<br>
A raincoat is all I need in life’s journey<br>

A cool spring breeze sobers up the spirit,<br>
a slight chill,<br>
Yet the setting sun over the mountains is welcoming.<br>
Looking back over this bleak path<br>
Were I to return<br>
I would heed not rain<br> 
nor shine.<br>
</div>
